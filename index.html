ORIGIN STORY

        
— how the operator works —

      


      

        

          /01 — METHOD
          

            Most software assumes the inputs are clean, the model is right, and the world will hold still long enough to ship.
          

        


        

          /02 — STAKES
          

            The Operator works downstream of that lie — in legal advice, in money, in health, in model readouts, in physical systems.
          

        


        

          /03 — CRAFT
          

            So every system is built around the verification, not the answer. Refuse when unsure. Simulate when reality is expensive.
          

        


        

          PRINCIPLES · 03
          
    
            
  1. Verify before answering. RAG + audit + refusal.
    2. 
            
  2. Simulate when reality costs. CFD, BEMT, walk-forward.
    3. 
            
  3. Refusal is a feature. Calibrated "I don't know" beats a confident hallucination.
    4.
